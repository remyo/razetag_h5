Capture with less typing.

Add a photo, read supported product-label text, or scan a barcode. RazeTag can remember barcode details you save for next time.

02

Every item, in its place.

Keep quantities, categories, condition, and status together. Find the right stock with search, filters, and photo-first inventory views.

03

One buy. Many possibilities.

Buy a bundle, track the items inside it, and sell them separately or together. Follow the purchase cost as you recover it.

04

Sell the way you work.

Record a single sale, sell part of a quantity, or combine items into a bundle sale. Keep returns and refunds in the picture, too.

05

Understand your numbers.

Follow recorded costs, sales, profit, and cash flow. Set a monthly buying limit to keep your next purchase in perspective.

06

Your next listing, ready.

Preview and copy a readable product listing. Then paste it into your preferred marketplace when you are ready to post.

Choose what goes.

Select the items you want to send, with their photos and listing details.

Keep purchase details optional.

Private purchase information is off by default when sharing selected items.

Review before saving.

The receiving device reviews the shared items and chooses what to accept.

Both devices need a compatible RazeTag version and the same Wi-Fi. Local network permissions may be required. Sharing is a deliberate transfer, not automatic synchronization.
