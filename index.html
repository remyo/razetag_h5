Local device sync

One inventory.
Your devices, together.

Add a find on your phone. Pick up the work on another phone or your Mac. Keep inventory, photos, and recorded sales in step over the same Wi-Fi, without a cloud account.

  • Same Wi-Fi
  • Two devices
  • Your approval

  1. Connect nearby

    Open Sync devices in Local network on both devices. Use the same Wi-Fi and read the full-inventory notice.

  2. Pair and approve

    Start on one device, join from the other, then approve. Both apps should be updated; a Mac joins using the nearby-device code.

  3. Keep both apps open

    Continue working during the session. Competing edits and deletions need review. Pair again after a session ends.

For devices you trust. Sync includes private purchase costs and financial history, not just public listing details. It is not cloud or background sync, and it does not replace a separate backup.

On your device. In your hands.

Your records
stay with you.

Your inventory and saved photos live on your device. Everyday tracking works offline. You decide when to make a backup, send selected items, or approve a sync with another device.

Sync is not a backup. Keep a separate copy somewhere safe in case a device is lost or replaced. Optional card lookups and fresh exchange rates need internet access.
