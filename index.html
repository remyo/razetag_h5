01 / Your first item

Start simple. Add details as you go.

You can use the + on Home, or open the Add tab. Manual entry works without camera access.

  1. Choose how to add

    Select Create details manually for a blank form, Take a photo for a guided reader, or Scan a barcode for a product code.

  2. Choose a category and name

    Pick the matching category, then enter the product name. Open more categories if the one you need is not in the quick list. Reader results are suggestions—check them before saving.

  3. Add photos

    Use the photo add tile to choose Camera or Gallery. Add more than one photo if you want to show the front, back, accessories, or defects.

  4. Set quantity and prices

    For an ordinary item, Purchase price and Planned selling price are per unit. Quantity 3 at ₱1,000 each means ₱3,000 purchase cost. Include relevant deductions, such as inbound shipping or repairs.

  5. Save and find it in Inventory

    Review the category-specific details and tap Save item. You can reopen the item to edit it, copy a listing, archive it, or record a sale. Taking a photo creates a draft; it does not save the item for you.

For card and label readers

Use good lighting, avoid glare, and keep the text inside the frame. Japanese and English text are supported. Move a little farther away if the camera cannot focus. Always review the suggested name.

For barcodes

A barcode is a product identifier, not a guaranteed online lookup. After you save a product with its barcode, RazeTag can learn it locally and prefill details when you scan it again.

Edit basics, quantity, and dates

Inventory → Item → Edit

Set the name, category, condition, status, currency, quantity, purchase price, and planned selling price. Currency and quantity share a row; they have different jobs. Enter the number of identical units in Quantity, not repeated copies of the same item.

For ordinary items, purchase and planned selling prices are per unit. Purchase bundles have different cost-allocation rules; see Purchase bundles.

Expand the optional sections for more information. Dates default from the phone and remain editable. Save changes with Save item; an unchanged edit does not need saving. If you leave with unsaved changes, choose whether to Keep or Discard.

Category-specific details

Add or Edit → Category-specific details

The default categories are Card, Booster packs & boxes, Game, Console, Figure, Handheld, Controller, and Other. Extra fields appear for the selected type:

  • Cards: set/expansion code, collector number, rarity, illustrator, language, translated name, and optional grading details.
  • Booster packs & boxes: franchise, set, set code, product type, language, pack count, and factory-sealed status.
  • Games: platform, edition, region, language, product code, and region code.
  • Consoles, handhelds, and controllers: package information and included accessories. Consoles/handhelds support Device only, With controller, With 2 controllers, Complete package, or Custom contents. Controllers have their own Controller only, With accessories, Complete in box, and Custom contents choices. Edit What is included? to match the actual package.
  • Figures: franchise, character, manufacturer, type, scale, and whether the box is included.
  • Custom categories: any extra fields you create in Settings.

Leave unreadable or unknown optional details empty rather than guessing. A card does not need a collector number to be saved. Use View more to see categories outside the quick choices.

Purchase information and private notes

Add or Edit → Purchase information

Expand purchase information to record where you bought the item, seller name/contact, order reference, warranty, and purchase notes. Open More details for Status, Storage location, Barcode / JAN, Serial number, Included accessories, Known defects, and Private notes. These help you prepare and find stock later.

Private purchase fields are excluded from the generated public listing. Free text and photos still need your review before you share them.

Deductions and the profit preview

Add or Edit → Additional costs / deductions → Add deduction

Add costs such as Inbound shipping, repair, cleaning, grading, or replacement parts. Choose the expense type (or enter a Custom name) and a positive amount. These expenses apply to the whole lot, not once per unit. They affect investment and profit. Form deductions are saved with Save item. On a saved item, Item expenses → Add expense can add/edit/delete expenses directly with Save expense.

Keep purchase-related expenses separate from sale-related fees, shipping, and packaging. Recording the same shipping charge in both places would deduct it twice. Use consistent currencies; a mismatch can make profit unavailable.

02 / Home

Your starting point.

Use the five bottom tabs—Home, Inventory, Add, Tools, Settings—to move around the app.

Understand the overview

Home → Overview

Invested is money tied up in active stock, including recorded item costs and expenses. Potential profit compares planned sale values with those costs; it is not money already earned. Active items and Listed describe your current inventory.

Totals use the currency shown on the card. They are not a live conversion of every currency in your database. A currency mismatch can make a cost or profit unavailable rather than produce a misleading total.

Tap Inventory to browse stock or Sales for recorded revenue and profit. Use the currency icon for Market & currency.

Add, scan, and hide totals

Home → Top-right icons

The + opens Take a photo, Add manually, or Purchase bundle. The scan icon opens the camera in QR mode; switch to Barcode for product codes.

The eye icon hides or shows Home money totals. If purchase-cost protection is enabled, revealing protected totals requires device authentication. Hiding totals does not remove any records.

Recently updated items

Home → Recently updated

Tap an item to open it, or View all to open Inventory. Home shows up to four recent active entries and avoids repeating the same purchase bundle. Sold items belong in the Sold filter and sales history.

Find stock by how long you have held it

Home → Inventory aging

Age is counted in calendar days from the purchase date, or the saved creation date when a purchase date is missing. The groups are 0–29, 30–59, 60–89, and 90+ days.

Only groups containing active items appear. Tap a group to open its items, ordered with the oldest first, then open an item for review. Empty inventory has no aging card.

03 / Inventory

Find it. Open it. Keep it organized.

Your item list, active statuses, sold records, and archive.

Find items and switch views

Inventory → Search, category choices, and filters

Search by item name, barcode, and saved product details such as set/collector codes, game regions, accessories, or storage location. The category choices narrow the same inventory view. Use the grid/list switch for photo cards or compact rows.

With an empty search, All shows active stock and excludes sold records. Choose Sold to browse completed sales. A text search can also find sold items, so check the status before opening a result. Archived records are in Archived.

Quick filters include In stock, Needs attention, Ready, Listed, and Reserved. Open the filter icon for category, storage location, currency/minimum whole-lot purchase cost, purchase-date range, and age: Any age, Up to 30 days, 31–90 days, or Over 90 days. These age ranges differ from Home’s aging groups. Use Apply to apply them or Reset to clear them. Protected purchase costs may need authentication before price filtering.

Open a product or a sold record

Inventory → Tap an item

An active item opens its detail page, not its edit form. Review photos, pricing, product information, costs, and buying checks; use Edit when you want to change something.

A sold item opens a read-only transaction record. Its related-record action leads to sale management when you need to correct or refund it. A combined sale opens the Bundle sale record first, so its member items stay together.

Immediately after saving a new item, Add another like this can start a similar draft. Review its quantity, costs, photos, and identifiers before saving.

Select several items at once

Inventory → Multi-select icon

Enter selection mode, then tap item checkboxes. Use Select all for the currently shown results, not every record hidden by your filters. The selection bar offers actions appropriate to those items.

Use bulk actions to change status, move storage location, archive, or start a bundle sale. Review the selected count and any confirmation before applying a change. Already-sold items have restrictions to protect their sale history.

To sell several selected products in one transaction, see Sell as a bundle.

Archive, restore, or delete a mistaken item

Inventory → Item → Archive; Inventory → Archived

Archive hides a record from active stock without erasing it. Open Archived to open archived items and restore them. You do not need to mark a mistakenly added product sold just to remove it.

To remove it permanently, archive it first, then delete it from Archive. Select a single item or several, use the delete icon, and read the confirmation. There is no Undo for permanent deletion.

Deleting an item can also delete linked photos, expenses, and transaction data, changing reports. Export a backup before a large cleanup. Use Clear cache when you only want to remove temporary files.

Permanent archive deletion is unavailable in Demo. Purchase-bundle children must be managed through their bundle. Deleting an archived sale-bundle member covers the whole transaction, and every member must first be archived.

04 / Photos & readers

Capture details with less typing.

Photograph products, scan labels, and prepare clear product photos. Always review reader suggestions.

Add photos, review them, and save a gallery copy

Add or Edit → Item photos

Tap the add-photo tile, then choose Camera or Gallery. Gallery can attach several selected images. Tap a thumbnail to preview it; the gallery-save action creates a separate copy on your phone. An already-saved copy may not need another save button.

Pending new photos have remove controls. Save the item to keep accepted changes. From item details, open the Photos viewer to swipe through pictures and pinch to zoom.

For receipts, use Purchase information → Add receipt photos from gallery. Receipts are private purchase information, not public product photos. Broken-photo references may offer removal from item details; removing a reference cannot recover a missing original file.

Read a card or photograph a product

Add → Take a photo

Choose Card, Console, or Others. The camera opens from the back of the phone. Use the light, zoom, and tap-to-focus controls when needed.

  • Card: keep it upright inside the outline with its name near the top-left. The live checker suggests readable text; you still capture and review it.
  • Console: frame the device and included accessories.
  • Others: frame the whole item in the square.

The photo opens a draft. Supported card/game text can fill blank fields; Console/Others capture is not guaranteed visual model identification. You can still capture when the live checker cannot find a name, then type it manually.

Japanese and English cards are supported, including cards without readable collector numbers. Translate with Google in card details offers a translated Japanese name: the language models need an initial download, then translation can work offline. Check the official product name yourself. Use Copy recognized text when offered to reuse OCR output.

Use the name inside the label frame

Add or Edit → Read label, beside Item name

  1. Point the camera at the intended product label inside the frame.
  2. Tap Read label.
  3. Review Detected text and edit Product name if needed.
  4. Choose OK, use this text to fill Item name, or Read again to retry.

The frame guides recognition toward the intended label. OCR can still make mistakes, especially with glare, blur, or multiple lines. This action fills the name after confirmation; it does not identify every detail automatically.

Read or type a product barcode

Add → Scan a barcode; Add or Edit → More details → Barcode / JAN

Scan a UPC/EAN/JAN-style product label. If it cannot be read, use Enter it manually, enter the Barcode number, and continue. Create without barcode opens a blank draft.

RazeTag can remember locally saved product identifiers and suggest a name/category on a later scan. An unknown barcode may leave details blank; review the category. Scanning starts a draft or fills the barcode field—it does not save the item or fetch a live market price.

Learned barcode/catalog data is separate from the individual inventory record and is included in full backups. Scanning a product barcode is different from scanning a RazeTag item QR label.

Remove the background and center a product

Add or Edit → Photo preview → Remove background

  1. Use a clear photo of one item, under 15 MB.
  2. Choose Remove background; processing runs on the device.
  3. Compare Original and Centered. Check edges and small accessories.
  4. Tap Use this photo, then Save item.

The centered result is a transparent PNG staged as a New cover photo. The original stays among the photos. Closing the editor without accepting does not replace it. If processing fails, use Try again or keep the original.

Use a photo’s date when available

New item → Photos → Dates

An imported older photo can suggest an acquisition date when readable metadata is available. Look for Suggested from photo and change it if the photo date is not the day you bought the item.

Not every image has usable metadata. Screenshots, edited images, or shared copies may not preserve the original date; use the phone default or enter the correct date.

05 / Purchase bundles

One purchase. Searchable children.

Use a purchase bundle for stock you bought together. It is different from a bundle sale.

Create a purchase with several child items

Add → Purchase a bundle; or Home → + → Purchase bundle

  1. Enter Bundle name, Currency, Total paid, and purchase date.
  2. Open More purchase details for source/store/platform, seller, order reference, and notes.
  3. Add at least two different item lines. Each has Item name, Category, Quantity, Estimated resale (each), and its own photos.
  4. Choose the cost-allocation method and check the total.
  5. Tap Create purchase bundle.

Use Quantity for identical units: 1,500 copies of the same product stay one line with quantity 1,500, not 1,500 listings. You can add up to 50 new lines per save. This is a line limit, not a 50-unit stock limit.

Each child is searchable in Inventory. Its photos belong to that child. Use the bottom Add another item action when entering a long bundle.

Use the same quantity or selling price for new lines

Purchase bundle form → Shared item values

Open Shared item values and enable Same quantity and/or Same selling price. Enter Quantity for every item or Selling price per unit.

These values apply to new draft lines, including lines you add afterward. Confirm before overwriting differing draft values. Turning a switch off makes individual line values editable again. These controls do not retroactively update saved children.

Example: keychain A, B, and C can each have quantity 100 and the same unit selling price, while remaining three distinct products.

Resale value, Equal, or Manual?

Purchase bundle form → Cost allocation

  • Resale value: weights each line by Estimated resale (each) × Quantity. More valuable lines receive more of the purchase cost.
  • Equal: splits equally between different item lines, regardless of quantity or target price.
  • Manual: you enter each allocated cost. The sum must equal Total paid exactly.

For a ₱6,000 bundle with two lines whose total resale estimates are ₱4,000 and ₱2,000: Resale value allocates ₱4,000/₱2,000; Equal allocates ₱3,000/₱3,000. If every resale estimate is zero, Resale value falls back to equal shares, explaining why the two can look identical.

Balanced confirms the split; Check total means you need to review it. A child’s allocated purchase cost belongs to its whole line—do not multiply it by quantity again. Its estimated selling price is still per unit.

Track recovery and sell remaining stock

Inventory → Purchase bundle

Review Original cost, Net sales, Remaining to recover, Net position, and recovery progress. Selling children reduces what remains to recover; it does not rewrite the original amount paid. Item expenses reduce net position separately.

Under Bundle item lines, choose All, Available, or Sold. Use View for a child or Sell for its sale dialog. With at least two eligible available lines, use Select items or Sell all N for a combined sale, even after other children have sold.

Inventory previews three active purchase bundles; View all opens the full Purchase bundles list. Bundles without available unarchived children are not in that active list.

Add, edit, or remove children safely

Purchase bundle → Add item or Edit

Add item opens bundle editing focused on new children. Edit opens the whole bundle. Save pending bundle changes before using Existing items → Review to edit or delete an existing child.

Deleting an existing child commits immediately after confirmation; it is not held until Save bundle changes and cannot be cancelled by leaving the form. It removes the child and its managed photos. The bundle must keep at least two item lines. Other bundle-form changes use Save bundle changes.

Before any child has sale history, the bundle’s total, currency, allocation, and contents can change. Once a child has sale history, those fields and contents are locked; shared descriptive purchase details can still be edited. Removing all eligible sale records is required before those financial/contents edits become available again.

Individual child editing still supports name, category, target price, photos, condition, and item-specific information. Shared purchase cost, currency, and acquisition information are managed through the purchase bundle. Existing child quantity cannot currently be edited in either editor; quantity controls apply to new draft lines.

Whole-bundle deletion is blocked while child sale history exists. Review the confirmation carefully; do not treat Delete as a reversible archive action.

06 / Sales & returns

Record the sale—and what happens next.

Track units sold, selling costs, refunds, and the stock that remains.

Sell all or part of one listing

Inventory → Active item → Mark as sold

  1. Enter Quantity sold when the listing contains several units.
  2. Enter Sold price or Total sold price: the buyer’s total payment for those units, not the unit price.
  3. Open More sale details for a preset, Sold through, Sale date, Fees, Shipping, Packaging, or Refund.
  4. Review and tap Save sale.

Example: you have 8 units and sell 3 at ₱1,200 each. Enter quantity 3 and total ₱3,600. Inventory keeps 5 available and records a separate sold lot of 3. Cost shares and saved expenses are split proportionately.

The date defaults to today and can be adjusted, but not into the future. Once recorded, sold quantity is not directly editable.

Sell several listings as a bundle

Home → Sales → Sell bundle; or Inventory → Multi-select → Sell bundle

  1. Search available items. Filter by All, Listed, Ready, or In stock if needed.
  2. Select at least two different listings in the same currency.
  3. Tap Review N items. Enter an optional Bundle name and the Total sold price.
  4. Review Sold through, Sale date, and Deductions and notes: Additional item costs, Additional fees, Shipping, Packaging, Refund, and Sale notes.
  5. Tap Save bundle sale.

This sells the whole quantity of every selected listing, not a chosen number of units per listing. Use an individual partial sale when you need to sell only some units.

The sale currency follows the selected stock. Price and deductions are allocated across members, preserving the exact transaction total. Existing item expenses are already included: do not enter the same expense again as an additional fee.

Correct a sale or undo a bundle sale

Sold record → Related item or Bundle sale → Edit

For a standalone sale, open the item editor and Sale details, correct prices, fees, dates, or channel, then Save item. Changing Status away from Sold asks Remove this recorded sale? Choose Keep sale or Remove sale and save.

A saved combined sale has Edit bundle sale and Save bundle changes. Its member list stays fixed while editing. Undo bundle sale removes the shared transaction and restores each member’s previous status after confirmation.

Undoing a mistaken record is different from returning money to a customer. Physically returned sales cannot be undone through the ordinary sale-removal workflow.

Refund money and, when eligible, return stock

Sold item → Refund

  1. Open the sold item’s management page and choose Refund.
  2. Enter Money returned in the original sale currency, from zero up to the original sold price.
  3. Choose whether to Return item to inventory, when that option is available.
  4. Confirm with Save refund or Refund & return.

Money-only: stock stays sold. Physical return: the entire sold lot returns as a separate active item with Returned status. Inspect its condition and update its status before selling again; the original sale remains in history.

There is no partial physical-return quantity control. Bundle-sale members and purchase-bundle children currently support money-only refunds, not physical restocking. A zero-money physical return is possible for eligible stock.

After a recorded refund/return, the separate Refund action is no longer offered. For physically returned stock, open the returned item → View refund details to see Return & refund history, then View refund transaction (or View original sale record for a zero-money return). Money-only refunds appear under Cash flow → Month → Expenses. An ordinary sale editor can still expose the recorded Refund amount for correction.

Read performance, charts, and profit

Home → Sales

Choose 7D, 30D, 90D, 1Y, or All to update performance totals, the chart, and recent sold records. The summary shows Gross sales, Refunds, Other deductions, Net proceeds, Net profit, and Sales.

  • Net sales = gross sales − refunds.
  • Net proceeds = gross sales − refunds − selling fees − shipping − packaging.
  • Net profit = net proceeds − realized purchase cost − saved item expenses.

Profit quality adds Gross margin, ROI, Avg profit / sale, and Avg days held. Bundles count as one sale transaction; a transaction count is not a count of individual units. Physical returns adjust realized costs so returned stock is not charged twice.

Financial totals use the selected app currency, without automatic conversion of all records. Incompatible cost currencies can make profit unavailable. Refunds in Sales adjust the original sale’s period; Cash flow tracks the outgoing money on its refund date.

Find older sold items

Sales → Sold items → See all

The Sales page previews the latest three transactions for its current view. When See all is available, it opens the complete Sold items history, not only the selected performance period.

Use Search sold items for names, categories, channel, currency, or bundle title. Sold records can also be found with Inventory’s Sold filter. A bundle record keeps the sale’s member listings together.

Permanent cleanup is separate: Settings → Delete sold data.

07 / Tools

Prepare your next sale.

Open a tool below to see how it works and whether it saves changes.

Tasks: what needs attention next

Tools → Tasks

Choose All, Attention, Details, or Selling. Tasks are generated from active inventory; there is no manual task form or “complete” checkbox. One listing gets its highest-priority task, regardless of quantity.

  • Attention: follow up a reservation, inspect a return, finish repair/testing/evaluation, or decide what to do with parts.
  • Details: add a usable photo, then set a planned selling price.
  • Selling: prepare a Ready item for listing.

Use Review, Add photo, Set price, or Open item, make the relevant change, and save. The queue updates from the record; resolving one issue can reveal the next. Sold and archived items are excluded.

Bundle planner: check a sale before committing

Tools → Bundle planner

  1. Search and select at least two complete available listings in one currency.
  2. Tap Review bundle; use Change to adjust the selection.
  3. Enter Bundle selling price, optionally choose a preset, and adjust Platform & payment fees, Shipping, Packaging, and Other deductions.
  4. Review Inventory cost, Recorded expenses, Total invested, Net proceeds, Expected profit, Margin / ROI, and Break-even price.
  5. Use Continue to bundle sale to transfer the scenario to the sale form, then review and save there.

The planner does not sell stock or save a history of plans. Break-even is invested cost plus the entered deductions; expected profit is net proceeds minus investment. Foreign-currency expenses make the result provisional. No live market-price service supplies these selling estimates.

Copy a public listing with the right details

Tools → Copy listing; or Item details → Copy icon

  1. Use Search items to copy and choose active stock.
  2. Review the Listing preview title and description.
  3. Toggle Emoji format, Selling price, and Warranty when those details exist.
  4. Use Copy title, Copy description, or Copy all, then paste into the marketplace yourself.

The listing can include condition, quantity, planned unit price, public category details, package contents, accessories, defects, and allowed custom fields. It excludes purchase costs, profit, seller information, storage location, serial numbers, private notes, and grading certificate numbers.

Review public text and photo backgrounds yourself. Copying does not publish an advert, copy all photos, or change the item to Listed. Save chosen photos separately and update its status after posting.

Create, save, and scan private item QR labels

Tools → QR labels; or eligible Item details → QR icon

  1. Use Search items for a QR label and select active stock.
  2. Review Item QR label, including the RazeTag mark and product title.
  3. Tap Save label to save a PNG to the RazeTag Labels album.
  4. Print that saved image using your phone’s normal gallery/printing options.

The visible label contains the title, category, and condition, not purchase or selling prices. The QR itself identifies a local item ID; it is not a public webpage and does not transfer the product database.

Scan through Home’s scan icon or the QR labels scanner. QR code opens matching active stock on that device; Barcode starts a new product draft with its code. Unknown, deleted, sold, or archived item labels cannot open as active stock. There is no built-in bulk print layout or printer setup.

Inspect an item before buying

Tools → Buying checklists

Use Search categories or checks, or expand a guide: Console, Handheld, Controller, Disc game, Card, Figure, Sealed product, or General item.

Tick checks as you inspect the real product, review the count, and use the reset icon to clear that guide. These ticks last only while the screen remains open; they do not save an inspection record.

The category checklist inside an item form/detail is an offline reference and is also not saved with the item. Record any important findings under defects or notes yourself. A checklist is not an automated controller or authenticity test.

08 / Budget & cash flow

Spending limits and cash movement.

Use Monthly budget to plan buying. Use Cash flow to inspect the movement of money.

Set a monthly buying limit and starting capital

Tools → Monthly budget

  1. Use the month arrows to select the period.
  2. Tap No spending limit or the spending-limit card.
  3. Choose Currency and enter Limit (optional). Leave it blank for a loose month, or use Remove limit.
  4. For starting capital, choose This month or Default, then enter Capital (optional).
  5. Tap Save budget plan.

A month-specific capital overrides the default. The default is reused for months without an override; it is not automatic carry-forward from last month. Use Clear month or Clear default to remove the appropriate capital setting.

The progress bar compares purchases with the optional limit. Selling items does not refill that spending allowance. Review Daily buying vs sales and Month activity: Purchases, Sales revenue, Realized profit, and Cash movement.

Capital remaining = starting capital − purchases this month. Sales/profit do not refill this number. For example, ₱60,000 capital and ₱57,000 purchases leave ₱3,000 here even if you sold stock during the month.

Changing currency does not convert typed amounts. Reports use the plan’s currency. Use Unlock figures if protected amounts are hidden.

See money coming in and going out

Tools → Cash flow

The dashboard shows the current month’s Estimated balance, Cash in, Cash out, and Net flow. Open a row under Monthly activity to inspect that month.

  • Cash in: recorded gross sales.
  • Cash out: purchases, item expenses, fees, shipping, packaging, and refunds.
  • Net flow: cash in minus cash out.
  • Estimated balance: matching starting capital plus net flow. Without capital, there is no balance estimate.

Purchase bundles count the original payment once, not again for every allocated child. Refunds appear as outgoing money on the refund date. Individual sale rows show proceeds after sale-time deductions, while the dashboard’s Cash in is gross sales. Figures use the selected currency and saved records; this is not a connected bank account or live cash balance.

This differs from Monthly budget’s Capital remaining, which only subtracts purchases. The budget’s Cash movement is also a simpler measure and does not include every separately dated expense that Cash flow shows.

Open a month and inspect a transaction

Tools → Cash flow → Month → Transaction detail

Filter the ledger by All, Sales, Purchases, or Expenses. Refunds appear under Expenses. Tap a transaction to view dates, quantities, amounts, cost breakdowns, and its related records.

Use Open related record or View bundle sale when available. Grouped transactions lead to the bundle before individual items. These transaction screens are read-only: corrections happen through the related item or sale, not by adding a separate wallet entry.

Deleting underlying inventory or sales can change these derived reports. Keep a backup or CSV before cleanup if you need an historical copy.

09 / Settings

Make RazeTag work for you.

Preferences are grouped into Inventory setup, Preferences, Privacy and data, and Help and testing.

Light, Dark, and accent colors

Settings → Appearance

Choose Light or Dark. Light is the default; the app does not automatically follow the phone’s system theme.

Choose an accent: Indigo, Cyan, Violet, or Emerald. It changes navigation, selections, and actions. Status and warning colors remain meaningful rather than all using the accent.

Choose the categories you see

Settings → Categories

Quick categories contains up to five choices shown directly on an item form. More categories appear through View more. Hidden categories are removed from the picker, not from saved records.

Use a category’s menu for Show in Quick, Show under More, or Hide from category picker. Promoting a sixth quick category moves the last other quick choice under More. Other always stays last and cannot be a Quick category.

Tap Reorder in Quick categories, drag the handles, then Done. Only the quick group is reorderable. Hiding Cards does not disable the Card camera reader.

Restore category defaults restores built-in placements and hides custom categories without deleting their records.

Create a category with your own icon and fields

Settings → Categories → Add category

  1. Enter Category name.
  2. Choose an Icon. Use View more → Choose an icon → Search icons for more choices.
  3. Choose an Accent color and where to Show category: Quick, More, or Hidden.
  4. Under Product details, use Add field when you need extra inputs.
  5. Save each field, then Save category.

You can keep up to 10 custom categories, including hidden ones, with up to 12 custom fields each. Photos, quantity, condition, and prices remain available without creating custom fields for them.

Fields support Text, Number, Yes / no, or Single choice. Single choice needs at least two distinct comma-separated choices. Enable Required if needed. Include in Copy Listing controls whether a field is public; turn it off for private information.

Save field or Cancel closes the inline field editor. Tap a saved field to edit it, or remove its definition from the draft. Built-in categories cannot be edited or deleted.

Delete an unused custom category

Settings → Categories → Category menu → Delete category

Deletion is blocked while any personal or demo inventory record still uses that category, including sold or archived items. Move those items to another category first, or keep the category hidden instead.

If only learned barcode suggestions use it, those suggestions move to Other when the category is deleted. Category deletion has no Undo. Hiding is the safer choice when you only want to simplify the picker.

Currency display, rate checks, and market region

Settings → Currency or Market & currency

Supported currencies are PHP, JPY, and USD; PHP is the default. Prices use whole currency units. Choosing a currency changes the display/new-item default, not the saved amounts of existing products.

In Market & currency, choose Display currency and Market region: Philippines, Japan, or United States. Automatic conversion can show estimates alongside supported inventory prices while preserving the original amount and currency.

  1. Under Currency rate check, select From.
  2. Enter the positive whole-number Amount, such as 3,000 PHP.
  3. Tap Check rate to compare it with the other two supported currencies.

Fresh rate checks require Internet. Saved daily rates can be reused offline; review Updated, Cached, Offline cache, and the rate date. These are conversion estimates, not guaranteed bank/payment rates.

Market region does not turn on live card, game, or console price lookup. Financial reports do not automatically combine all currencies just because display conversion is on.

Save selling-channel fee assumptions

Settings → Selling-channel presets → Selling channels

Use Use selling-channel presets to show or hide presets in supported sale/planning forms. Turning it off preserves your saved assumptions and still allows manual fees and channel entry.

Edit Facebook Marketplace, Carousell, eBay, or Local meetup. Set Platform fee %, Payment fee %, Cost currency, Fixed fee, Packing, and Shipping allowance, then Save preset.

Fees combine the two percentages of sale price, rounded to whole units, plus the fixed fee. These values are your assumptions, not platform fees fetched online. Review the applied numbers for each sale. A selected preset can be deselected to use manual entry.

Restore preset defaults replaces edited assumptions after confirmation. There is no custom add/delete/rename preset control.

Lock the app and protect sensitive actions

Settings → App protection

Enable Lock RazeTag to authenticate when opening or returning to the app. Set up Face ID, fingerprint, or the supported phone PIN/passcode first. RazeTag uses the device’s authentication and does not store your biometric data or passcode.

To turn it off, confirm Turn off app protection? → Authenticate. Keep protection or cancelled/failed authentication leaves it enabled; it does not count as a successful unlock.

Optional controls:

  • Hide money totals on Home masks money, not item counts.
  • Authenticate for backup protects full backup creation/restoration.
  • Authenticate for permanent deletion protects confirmed destructive cleanup.
  • Authenticate to view purchase costs keeps private costs hidden until unlocked.
  • Encrypt new backups protects newly exported backup files with a separate password.

Changing an authentication guard requires authentication. A successful sensitive-action unlock can last up to two minutes and ends when the app becomes inactive. App lock and backup encryption do not encrypt exported CSV reports or turn the local database into an encrypted database.

Measure storage and clear only temporary cache

Settings → RazeTag-managed data → Storage

Review Saved photos, Databases, Temporary cache, and their file counts. Use the refresh icon to measure again if some files were busy or unavailable.

Clear cache → Clear temporary cache? → Clear cache removes rebuildable temporary files only. It preserves saved item photos, inventory, sales, archives, and databases.

Manage sold items and Manage archive open separate record-deletion workflows. They are not cache clearing.

The total covers RazeTag-managed data, not all phone storage. It excludes the app binary, OS data, downloaded ML models, gallery copies, and external backups. After deleting records, SQLite may retain freed space for reuse, so its file size may not shrink immediately.

Permanently delete selected sold records

Settings → Delete sold data

  1. Choose All sold, Older than 30 days, Older than 90 days, Older than 1 year, or Custom dates. These use the sold date, not the purchase date.
  2. Optionally use Search sold items to delete.
  3. Select records or use Select all for matching results. Changing filters/search clears the selection.
  4. Tap Review N selected and check the names and sold dates.
  5. Confirm Delete permanently and authenticate if protected.

Selecting a member of a sale bundle selects the entire transaction, including members outside the visible filter. Review the confirmation’s full list.

This deletes the selected inventory, sale data, expenses, linked records, and app-managed photos. There is no Undo; recovery requires a previous backup. Gallery copies and external backups stay intact. The action is disabled while Demo is open.

Practise with Demo and generate test listings

Settings → Demo inventory → Open demo

Demo uses separate sample inventory and sales. The demo banner tells you when it is active. Use Close demo to return to personal inventory. Demo changes persist for later sessions; closing it is not a reset.

While Demo is active, open Performance test items. Choose 100, 200, or 3,000, or enter Number of listings from 1 to 5,000 per run, then Add test items. These are lightweight text-only records for scrolling/search testing. Repeat if needed for a larger demo total.

Your personal inventory is not changed by test generation. Settings can still affect the app, and backups and CSV exports always use personal data, even with Demo open. There is no visible reset-demo action.

See how data and photo reading stay on the phone

Settings → On this device

This informational section explains Local database, Saved item photos, On-device photo reader, and Automatic translation. Core inventory uses local SQLite storage, with app-managed saved photos.

Google ML Kit reads supported card/game text without uploading the photo for recognition. Translation models need an initial download; suggestions still need review. “On-device” does not mean every feature avoids Internet—fresh exchange-rate checks need a connection.

10 / Your data

A backup is your safety net.

RazeTag is local-first. There is no automatic cloud account to sign back into if you lose or reset your phone.

BEFORE CHANGING PHONES

Export a backup

  1. For password protection, first enable Settings → App protection → Encrypt new backups.
  2. Open Settings → Backup and reports → Backup & restore.
  3. Tap Create backup. If encryption is enabled, choose a password of at least 8 characters.
  4. Save the .razetag file outside the app and keep a second copy before uninstalling or clearing app data.

Backups are designed to move your supported app data and saved photos between Android and iOS. A CSV report is for reading, not restoring.

RESTORE REPLACES DATA

Restore carefully

Open Settings → Backup and reports → Backup & restore → Restore backup. Read Replace personal inventory?, tap Choose file and replace, and select the .razetag file. If encrypted, enter its Backup password and tap Unlock backup. Cancelling or using an invalid file/wrong password does not replace inventory.

Restoring replaces personal inventory; it does not merge two inventories. Read the confirmation and back up the destination phone first if it already has records you want to keep.

An encrypted backup’s password cannot be recovered by the app. Keep it somewhere safe.

Never share your personal backup with the APK.

An unencrypted backup can contain private prices, seller information, records, and photos. Share the installer and this guide—not your real inventory database or backup. Backups always use personal inventory, even while Demo is open.

What a full backup contains

Settings → Backup and reports → Backup & restore

A .razetag backup includes personal inventory, purchase and sale bundles, sale/refund records, item expenses, saved photos, catalog products and learned barcodes, and saved inspection/listing records where present.

It also includes supported preferences such as appearance, accent, currency, custom categories/fields, selling presets, monthly-budget plans, and selected privacy/display preferences.

Demo inventory, device app-lock/authentication settings, downloaded models, separate gallery exports, and external backup copies are not included. Restore leaves Demo and device authentication settings unchanged; review restored appearance and other included preferences afterward.

Use an encrypted backup

Settings → App protection → Encrypt new backups

After enabling this, Create backup asks for Backup password and Confirm password. Use at least eight characters; both entries must match. Store the password somewhere safe because RazeTag does not retain or recover it.

Encryption applies to newly created full backups only. It does not change old backup files, CSV exports, or the on-device database. An encrypted restore asks for the backup password; this is different from your device unlock.

Export readable inventory and sales reports

Settings → Backup and reports → CSV reports

Choose Inventory CSV or Sales CSV, then save through Files. Open the result in a spreadsheet app. Inventory CSV includes archived records; Sales CSV includes sale/bundle amounts, deductions, refund details, and profit.

CSV reports contain private purchase-cost/profit information even though they omit seller contacts, serials, private notes, and photo paths. They are unencrypted and always use the personal database, even in Demo.

CSV is for reading and analysis, not restoring RazeTag. The “Authenticate for backup” option is not a guarantee that CSV export is authenticated. Keep private reports separate from public listing text.

11 / Phone permissions

Allow what you use.

You do not need to grant every permission to manage inventory. The prompts depend on your phone, operating system, and the feature you open.

Camera When scanning or taking photos

Used for barcodes, QR codes, card and label readers, and new product photos.

If declined: you can still create items manually and use selected gallery photos.

Photos / Gallery When choosing or saving photos

The system picker lets you select images to attach. Saving a copy to the RazeTag album on iOS requests Photos read/write access; it is not an add-only export. You do not need to grant full-library access just to choose a photo.

If declined: existing saved inventory and its app-managed photos remain usable; gallery export may be unavailable.

Device authentication Optional protection

Used when you enable App protection or protect sensitive actions. The phone handles Face ID, fingerprint, or its supported device-lock method.

If not enabled: the app remains usable, but those optional protections are not active.

Files & backups Choose a file or destination

The phone’s file picker is used to open a backup or choose where to save an export. This is not permission to browse all files on your phone.

If cancelled: that import or export does not proceed.

Older Android storage Android 7–10 gallery export

These older Android versions may ask for storage access when saving photos to the gallery. Android 11 and newer do not use this old permission for gallery export.

If declined: you can keep using inventory, but gallery export may not work.

Internet Optional online features

Model downloads and exchange-rate refreshes need a connection. Android’s Internet permission is not a normal “Allow / Deny” runtime popup.

Offline: your saved inventory, quantities, photos, sales, and local tools remain available.

Microphone is not needed for these features.

The current Android APK declares microphone permission through its camera dependency, but RazeTag’s photo readers disable audio. You can leave microphone access denied; it is not required for inventory, photos, or scanning.

Change your mind later?

On Android, open Settings → Apps → RazeTag → Permissions. On iPhone, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera / Photos. Names vary by phone. Enable only the access needed for the feature you want to use.

12 / Good to know

A few common questions.

Can I keep using the app without Internet?

Yes, for the core inventory and sales workflows. Some smart features need an initial model download, and fresh exchange rates need Internet. Review reader suggestions: photo recognition is not guaranteed identification or automatic live market pricing.

Will an update delete my items?

A compatible in-place app update should keep local data. Back up first. Do not uninstall RazeTag or clear its storage to “make an update work”—that can remove local records and photos. Ask the person sharing the app if Android reports a package or signing conflict.

Why does a product not appear in my inventory?

Clear search and filters, check Sold and Archive, and confirm whether Demo inventory is open. A sold or archived item is not the same as a missing database record.

The reader found the wrong name. What should I do?

Retake the picture in even light, avoid glare, keep the intended text inside the frame, or enter the name manually. A reader suggestion should always be checked before saving. For close-up text, move back slightly if the camera is blurred.

Can my friends see my items by scanning a printed QR label?

A RazeTag item QR label identifies a locally saved item; it is not a public web listing or a cloud-sharing feature. The device scanning it needs the matching record. Use Copy listing and selected photos when you want to post an item publicly.

Why can’t I find Buying assistant, Stock audit, or Alerts?

Buying assistant and Stock audit are currently hidden. Alerts screens exist in the project but are not linked from the current app navigation. They are not part of this guide’s active workflows. Live product-price tracking, controller diagnostics, a public storefront, and cloud inventory sharing are not available through these tools.

Is this HTML file another version of the app?

No. This is an offline feature guide with real demo screenshots. Follow its instructions in RazeTag; this page does not access your inventory or perform sales, backups, or deletions.
